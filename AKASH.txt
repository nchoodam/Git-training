Akash Missile System: India’s Indigenous Air Defence Shield
The Akash Missile System is a medium-range surface-to-air missile (SAM) developed by India's Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) as part of the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP). It is designed to protect vulnerable areas and vulnerable points from aerial threats such as fighter jets, cruise missiles, air-to-surface missiles, and ballistic missiles.

Development and Background
The development of the Akash missile system began in the late 1980s under the IGMDP, which also led to the creation of other key Indian missile systems like Agni, Prithvi, Trishul, and Nag. The objective was to achieve self-reliance in missile technology and reduce dependency on foreign defense imports. The Akash missile was developed by DRDO in collaboration with Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL), and other Indian industries.

After years of development and rigorous testing, the Akash system was inducted into the Indian Armed Forces. The Indian Air Force (IAF) inducted it in 2014, followed by the Indian Army in 2015.

Technical Features
Range: The Akash missile can engage targets up to 30 km away and at altitudes from 30 meters to 18 km.

Speed: It travels at supersonic speeds, approximately Mach 2.5.

Warhead: Equipped with a 60 kg high-explosive, pre-fragmented warhead.

Guidance System: It uses a combination of command guidance and active radar homing.

Mobility: The system is fully mobile, mounted on platforms such as the Tatra truck, and can be rapidly deployed.

The system includes a multifunction radar called Rajendra, which is a passive electronically scanned array (PESA) radar capable of tracking up to 64 targets and engaging 12 of them simultaneously.

Operational Capabilities
The Akash system is capable of operating in all weather conditions and during day or night. It can neutralize aerial targets with high maneuverability and provides area air defense to mobile and static assets. It is considered one of the few systems in the world with such capability and mobility combined.

Export Potential and Strategic Impact
India has successfully tested and deployed the Akash system along its borders with Pakistan and China. Given its proven reliability, there has been increasing interest from several countries in Africa and Southeast Asia. In 2021, India approved the export of the Akash system under its "Atmanirbhar Bharat" (Self-Reliant India) initiative.

Upgraded Variants
Akash-1S: An improved version with an indigenous seeker for better accuracy and precision.

Akash-NG (Next Generation): Currently under development, this version aims to reduce the system’s weight, increase reaction time, and improve lethality with an active electronically scanned array (AESA) radar.

Conclusion
The Akash missile system is a significant milestone in India's journey toward self-reliance in defense technology. It showcases India's growing prowess in missile development and provides the Indian armed forces with a robust and reliable air defense shield. With ongoing upgrades and export interest, the Akash system continues to play a crucial role in India's strategic defense framework.
